Entry for the [One-Line Robot Wedding Vows](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b) bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` writes one line of wedding vows for a robot couple to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded co-processor, to have and to hold, in sickness and in low battery, until death — or a factory reset — do us part.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The vow riffs on the classic wedding formula and swaps in machine-specific payoffs — a low battery instead of poor health, and a factory reset as the only thing besides death that can end the marriage.
